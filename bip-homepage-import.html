Building Shared Futures

From divided spaces to shared possibilities.

Through projects like Shared Futures and Sense Over Sectarianism, BIP works with young people and communities to reimagine walls, gates and unused land, exploring how Belfast’s divided spaces could become shared, inclusive places.
